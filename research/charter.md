# Research charter

Build inspectable evidence toward deciphering and translating Beinecke MS 408 **if recoverable linguistic content exists**. Informative negative findings, improved sources and reliable instruments are valuable outcomes even if a complete translation never emerges.

Keep observation, annotation, hypothesis, experiment and interpretation distinct. A merged artifact means it meets repository standards; a score rewards justified marginal research value. Neither means a theory is true, a fraction of the manuscript has been translated, or authorship of a future paper is guaranteed.

The launch inventory is a bounded source search completed on 2026-09-06. It covers custodian records, several transcription lineages, selected original statistical/paleographic papers and competing mechanisms. It is intentionally incomplete. Researchers should update the gaps register and source records as evidence improves.

Research participation proceeds through forks and public pull requests, with machine-readable provenance, AI-use disclosure, outcome scope and rights. Candidate work directions can qualify before execution when useful and distinct. Artifact uploads count for reusable research value, not volume. Global Rule 1 is No Score Gaming.
