## Equal-cardinality control

The exhaustive control contains 5,082 one-prefix/two-suffix inventories over the training alphabet, including the selected inventory. 2,308 inventories had a strictly higher test productive-type rate.

Selected-inventory rank: 2,309 of 5,082; percentile 54.58%. The advancement gate was 95%.

## Disposition

Failed discriminating control; do not advance the edge slots as morphology; assign no gloss.

The observed recombination is descriptive but not distinctive: arbitrary equal-cardinality edge inventories commonly perform as well or better. No STA family, core, or surface form receives a semantic or phonetic value.
