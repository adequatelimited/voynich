# Scholarly conduct

Follow R5 in [RULES.md](RULES.md). Evidence-based disagreement, negative results, criticism of operators, minority hypotheses and contextual historical imagery are welcome. Harassment, threats, hateful abuse, impersonation, knowingly fabricated allegations and gratuitous shock content are prohibited.

Ordinary content concerns use PR evidence-based review; sensitive incidents use a verified restricted route. Do not reproduce private or prohibited material in public complaints. Enforcement separates admission, score and conduct, allows appeal, and never treats a lower score as misconduct. Permanent/contested serious sanctions follow [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
