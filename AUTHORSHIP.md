# Publication and research recognition

Points recognize useful work. They do not guarantee a translation, paper, byline, author position or control of a direction. Merging a hypothesis accepts repository material without endorsing its truth.

Record actual contribution/publication roles with consent. Authorship considers meaningful relevant contribution, drafting/substantive revision where applicable, approval of the submitted manuscript, accountability, conflict disclosure and the selected venue's policy. CRediT roles describe work without deciding authorship. Agree a fair byline/order before submission; a consortium/contributor appendix may be appropriate where permitted.

Historical authors receive scholarly attribution independently of GitHub. Routine service/support receive consent-aware acknowledgment. AI tools cannot accept authorship accountability. Do not invent publication roles or send invitations without actual consent/authorization. A legal name and biography are optional.

A candidate translation requires an explicit independently reproducible method, coverage/uncertainty and real independent external assessment under the solution protocol. Attractive prose, isolated matches, model confidence and high points are insufficient.
