# Source-specific rights

The exact downloaded ZL3b and GC2a transliterations retain their original headers and comments. [voynich.nu's licensing statement](https://voynich.nu/roadmap.html), inspected 2026-09-06, explicitly releases its hosted transliteration tables under **CC0**. Attribute ZL to René Zandbergen with Gabriel Landini's role preserved, and GC to Glen Claston with the hosted IVTFF conversion credited to René Zandbergen. This does not license the site's third-party fonts or make converted reading lineages independent datasets.

`pg11.txt` is the complete downloaded Project Gutenberg eBook 11, *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland* by Lewis Carroll; e-text credits include Arthur DiBianca and David Widger. Its [item record](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/11) labels the work public domain in the USA. Its source wrapper and terms are retained unchanged. The analytical code extracts only the marked book body; readers elsewhere should inspect applicable local terms.

Hashes, retrieval origins and byte lengths are recorded in [data/manifests](../manifests). These files retain their own source-specific terms, separate from the project's software and prose licenses. Original Takahashi HTML pages, manuscript images, scholarly PDFs and source fonts have not been imported.
