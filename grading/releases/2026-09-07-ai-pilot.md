# Scored challenge launch

**Scoring is live. Accepted contributions earn official points and appear on the weekly, monthly and all-time leaderboards. Calibration will be performed later; it is not a prerequisite for awarding points now.**

The founding operator authorized best-effort, fully AI grading, research merges and signed leaderboard credit under the published rubric. No human reference panel or routine human grading appointment is required. Contributors receive a recorded assessment, and accepted contributions receive the credit supported by that assessment and the family-credit rules.

## Current grading procedure

The active Sonnet profile and public methodology govern every submission. The current v2 profile uses two independent assessment contexts, an adjudicator when needed, and one bounded output corrector only if the adjudicator response fails validation. See the [output reliability release](2026-09-07-output-correction.md). The adversary cannot see the first assessment. Original responses, errors and receipts are retained. Tools and paid API fallbacks remain disabled.

The corrector cannot raise a score, promote a held decision to acceptance or remove an existing blocker. Valid decisions never trigger another random sample. The complete final schema, evidence citations, trusted receipt namespaces and universal gates must pass before acceptance. Missing required research evidence receives actionable revision feedback; a software/model-output failure is an operational hold, not a score of zero or a finding of misconduct.

## Points and publication

Successful trusted admission and repository checks allow a research PR to merge. The signed score decision then settles the contributor's points and publishes the leaderboard entry. While that separate publication is finishing, `accepted_score_pending` means the award is being settled; it does not mean scoring is disabled or waiting for calibration.

Family totals follow the published cumulative-credit rule across PRs. Supporting files and repeated outcomes do not multiply points. Scores recognize useful contribution value; they do not certify a translation or guarantee paper authorship. Weekly, monthly and all-time standings use verified earning dates.

A sole PR author assigning 100% to their own GitHub identity may acknowledge responsibility through the contribution template's rights and responsibility declarations. Other beneficiaries acknowledge their own shares. Unacknowledged shares remain withheld from ranking until acknowledged.

## Later calibration

The 36-case calibration study remains unfinished. It will measure and improve consistency after launch. We do not claim that study has passed, and we do not withhold current earned awards pending it. Future methodology changes are public and versioned, preserve the assessment history and follow the published correction and family-credit rules.

## Founding authorization

The operator explicitly authorized launching the scored challenge and publishing pending contributors' accepted work and scores. That founding authorization replaced the draft seven-day/two-human appointment requirement. This administrative release defines operating policy; all research scores still come from the AI assessment and deterministic settlement procedure.
