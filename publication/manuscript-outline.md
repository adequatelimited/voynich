# Prospective research publication outline

1. Mission, uncertainty and manuscript/source provenance.
2. Corpus encodings, segmentation assumptions, access and rights.
3. Declared protocols, controls and complete material attempt scope.
4. Results and independent reproduction, including negative findings.
5. Competing explanations, selection effects and limitations.
6. Reusable methods/data and scoped implications.
7. Human contributions, AI assistance, consent, conflicts and scholarly attribution.

This is an outline, not a completed paper, solved manuscript or guaranteed future byline. Source acquisition and code authorship do not automatically confer all underlying scientific discoveries.
