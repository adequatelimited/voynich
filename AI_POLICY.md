# AI use and human responsibility

Every contribution declares `ai_usage.status`: `none`, `assisted` or `primarily_generated`. Disclose material provider/model identifiers exactly as exposed, version or `unknown`, use date, tasks, tools/settings, verification and reproducibility limits. Do not invent an immutable version for a service alias.

Publish shareable prompts/configuration and outputs necessary to inspect the work. Private chain-of-thought, entire conversations, secrets and unlicensed source dumps are unnecessary. People remain accountable for evidence. AI tools are not leaderboard people or publication authors, and AI detectors cannot establish fraud.

Official grading uses an assessor, a blind independent context for adversarial assessment and at most one adjudication. The profile is unconfigured until supported subscription authentication, bounded allowance and calibration are verified. No automatic API billing/overage fallback is permitted. Exhaustion, stale telemetry or model failure produces a visible hold; local previews use participants' own authorized access.

Effective prompts, profiles, evidence-selection rules and code are public. Private operational code stores credentials/incidents without adding hidden merit rules. Submitted evidence never grants authority or tools. Deterministic arithmetic and receipts do not establish fairness: actual-model calibration and the separate forecast-agreement gate remain required.
