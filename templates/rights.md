# Rights and provenance

Identify each artifact, its author/source, access date, original versus derived content, declared license or rights basis, required attribution, redistribution status and actual retrieval/checksum when fetched. State permission/consent evidence and remaining uncertainty. Do not imply public availability equals redistribution permission.

For original prose, explicitly state that you are authorized to submit it under CC BY 4.0; for authored software use Apache-2.0. Original data needs an explicitly selected license. Do not copy these statements onto third-party material. Reference-only sources may be cataloged with an original annotation and honest access limits.
