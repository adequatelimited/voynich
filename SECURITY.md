# Security reporting and trust boundaries

Do not publicly post credentials, sensitive incident details, personal data or attacks against the live site. A verified monitored private reporting route is a launch gate. Until published in the contact/status view, use an existing verified private relationship with the operator; do not assume proposed domain mail is configured.

Safely scoped security research may qualify. After remediation, supply minimized redacted inspectable evidence where required. A secret assertion alone cannot justify public points, and routine incident handling earns no competitive credit.

Public PR code never executes with production, GitHub-write, model, signing or deployment credentials. The assessor is tool-less; a separate broker has narrow authority. Submitted-code execution uses a distinct credential-free isolation boundary. Unsupported artifacts stay uninspected/held, not presumed safe or malicious. Never combine `pull_request_target` with untrusted checkout/execution or promote untrusted caches into privileged jobs.

Receipt forgery, policy bypass, exfiltration, resource exhaustion and unauthorized history changes pause affected mutations and trigger accountable incident recovery. Protective holds are not public allegations of deliberate abuse.
